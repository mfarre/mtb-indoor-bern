## El Tony pilot

The [El Tony pilot](el-tony-pilot.md) improves a 340 m section of the Gurten course, from km 4.97 to km 5.31. Its ground materials use the following Poly Haven assets:

| Asset | Source and author | Maps used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `brown_mud_leaves_01` | [Brown Mud Leaves 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_mud_leaves_01), Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven | 1K JPG diffuse colour, OpenGL normal and roughness; woodland floor and margins |
| `aerial_ground_rock` | [Aerial Ground Rock](https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_ground_rock), Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven | 1K JPG diffuse colour, OpenGL normal and roughness; compacted trail and earth features |

Poly Haven distributes these assets under [CC0 1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license). The repository bundles the material maps, not Poly Haven website screenshots or preview renders. [The asset manifest](../src/MTBIndoorBern/Scene/assets/el-tony/sources.json) records source and download URLs, file sizes and SHA-256 hashes. The textures are generic materials, not photographs taken on Gurtentrail. Their tiling scale and colour treatment are visual choices; normal maps change surface lighting rather than route elevation or physical grip.

The [trailnet opening photographs](https://www.trailnet.ch/de/aktuell/recap---opening-gurtentrail-2-0) and [TRIEBHAUS installation photographs](https://www.triebhaus.ch/projekte/gurtentrail) guide the appearance of the surroundings and branding. They are not redistributed as textures. Sign and obstacle placement is approximate; the result is a recreation, not photogrammetry. Existing map and terrain provenance is described in the [Gurten guide](GURTEN.md).

The pilot's branching tree geometry, leaf cards, fern texture, shrubs, stones, fallen branches, leaf litter and contact shadows are generated in [the pilot module](../src/MTBIndoorBern/Scene/el-tony-pilot.js). Placement uses a fixed seed. No third-party tree models or generated photographic tree images are added by this pilot. [The scope module](../src/MTBIndoorBern/Scene/el-tony-profile.js) keeps the detailed treatment around the selected section, with transitions at its ends. Optional texture-loading failures fall back to existing generated textures so an unavailable material cannot prevent the course from opening.

## Audio assets

The ride audio uses original deterministic procedural buffers generated by [ride-audio-synthesis.js](../src/MTBIndoorBern/Scene/ride-audio-synthesis.js), currently version 3. Seven continuous sources provide tyre, wind, braking and corner-scrub sounds. A short one-shot gear click follows a host-confirmed successful shift; continuous chain/freehub buzz remains removed. There are no bundled field recordings or third-party sound samples. New offline comparison exports use the same [Web Audio graph](../src/MTBIndoorBern/Scene/ride-audio.js) as the app. Historical El Tony media retains its version 1 soundtrack; the Dentenberg woodland comparison retains version 2 without drivetrain sound. These published videos have not been re-dubbed when the current graph changed. See [audio reproduction and verification](../tools/ride-audio/README.md).

## Dentenberg autumn textures

The local installation contains `src/MTBIndoorBern/Scene/courses/al-dente/textures/forest-floor.jpg`, a sample extracted from the owner's recording after rotating it 180°. This asset remains private and is excluded from the public repository. The renderer corrects broad lighting bands in the crop and tiles it across the ground. The local asset's origin and crop are recorded in `appearance.json`. The new public material files are kept in a separate directory and do not replace this private texture.

| Public fallback | Source and author | Maps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `forest_floor` | [Forest Floor](https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_floor), eye-candy.xyz / Poly Haven | Unmodified 1024×1024 JPG diffuse colour, OpenGL normal and roughness |

The three maps are distributed under [CC0 1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license). [Their manifest](../src/MTBIndoorBern/Scene/assets/dentenberg/sources.json) contains the original download URLs, dimensions, file sizes, MD5 values checked against the publisher's metadata and independently computed SHA-256 hashes. The [licence note](../src/MTBIndoorBern/Scene/assets/dentenberg/LICENSE.md) links the legal code. They are generic autumn material samples, not photographs of Dentenberg; the renderer's tint and tiling are visual choices. These files add no owner recording pixels to the public repository.

The [woodland renderer](../src/MTBIndoorBern/Scene/dentenberg-woodland.js) gives the existing private video floor precedence, with a small bump effect derived from the same colour image. The stock colour/normal/roughness set is used when the private image is absent; the unrelated stock normal is not mixed over private video colour. Material tiling is approximately 2.1 m. If the stock files also fail to load, the existing generated floor remains the final fallback. The existing beech foliage asset is reused when available; it is generated artwork already included in the repository. Nearby branching tree variants and undergrowth are assembled in code.

The reference review used the already corrected private DJI frames and Track Lab contact sheets. The observed woodland has tall pale trunks, mixed darker conifers and saplings, an airy ochre canopy, brown leaf cover and patches of dark earth, with cooler green undergrowth in shade. The open gravel approach remains visually distinct from the narrow leaf-covered forest trail. The central section marked `inferred` in `appearance.json` follows a different route from the recording: its atmosphere may guide the reconstruction, but unique trees, stumps and obstacles are not transferred as measured landmarks. See [Dentenberg visual scope](dentenberg-visuals.md).

`src/MTBIndoorBern/Scene/courses/al-dente/textures/beech-branch.png` is an RGBA texture generated with the integrated ImageGen tool for the recreated trees and shrubs. It is neither a photograph nor a measurement of the route's exact vegetation. The alpha channel is preserved. The elevation profile and trail position do not depend on this texture.

Prompt used:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: transparent alpha foliage card texture for a real-time mountain biking game set in Bern woodland in late autumn. Generate ONE botanically plausible natural irregular spray of thin beech twigs with many small crisp individual ochre, muted golden yellow, tan and brown beech leaves, a few desaturated olive leaves, loosely branching in all directions from a fine central woody branch. Front orthographic view; flat soft overcast illumination, no baked directional cast shadow; realistic leaf veins, fine serrated edges and variation, photographic natural detail. An irregular airy silhouette, visible transparent gaps between groups and individual leaves; no circular blob outline. The spray occupies 85% of a square image with generous transparent margins. No trunk, no full tree, no scenery, no ground, no objects, no text or labels. Truly transparent background, preserve alpha. Avoid luminous saturation, illustration, cartoon, repeated identical leaves.

Outside the licensed ground maps described above, the remaining vegetation, asphalt and gravel textures are generated in code. Estimated widths, season and sections can be adjusted in `appearance.json`. Individual obstacles seen on a section that does not match the course have not been transferred as though their positions were surveyed.
