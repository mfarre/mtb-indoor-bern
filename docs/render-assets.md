## El Tony pilot

The [El Tony pilot](el-tony-pilot.md) improves a 340 m section of the Gurten course, from km 4.97 to km 5.31. Its ground materials use the following Poly Haven assets:

| Asset | Source and author | Maps used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `brown_mud_leaves_01` | [Brown Mud Leaves 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_mud_leaves_01), Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven | 1K JPG diffuse colour, OpenGL normal and roughness; woodland floor and margins |
| `aerial_ground_rock` | [Aerial Ground Rock](https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_ground_rock), Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven | 1K JPG diffuse colour, OpenGL normal and roughness; compacted trail and earth features |

Poly Haven distributes these assets under [CC0 1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license). The repository bundles the material maps, not Poly Haven website screenshots or preview renders. [The asset manifest](../src/MTBIndoorBern/Scene/assets/el-tony/sources.json) records source and download URLs, file sizes and SHA-256 hashes. The textures are generic materials, not photographs taken on Gurtentrail. Their tiling scale and colour treatment are visual choices; normal maps change surface lighting rather than route elevation or physical grip.

The [trailnet opening photographs](https://www.trailnet.ch/de/aktuell/recap---opening-gurtentrail-2-0) and [TRIEBHAUS installation photographs](https://www.triebhaus.ch/projekte/gurtentrail) guide the appearance of the surroundings and branding. They are not redistributed as textures. Sign and obstacle placement is approximate; the result is a recreation, not photogrammetry. Existing map and terrain provenance is described in the [Gurten guide](GURTEN.md).

The pilot's branching tree geometry, leaf cards, fern texture, shrubs, stones, fallen branches, leaf litter and contact shadows are generated in [the pilot module](../src/MTBIndoorBern/Scene/el-tony-pilot.js). Placement uses a fixed seed. No third-party tree models or generated photographic tree images are added by this pilot. [The scope module](../src/MTBIndoorBern/Scene/el-tony-profile.js) keeps the detailed treatment around the selected section, with transitions at its ends. Optional texture-loading failures fall back to existing generated textures so an unavailable material cannot prevent the course from opening.

## Audio assets

The ride audio uses original deterministic procedural buffers generated by [ride-audio-synthesis.js](../src/MTBIndoorBern/Scene/ride-audio-synthesis.js), currently version 1. There are no bundled field recordings or third-party sound samples. The same [Web Audio graph](../src/MTBIndoorBern/Scene/ride-audio.js) is used in the app and in offline comparison exports. See [audio reproduction and verification](../tools/ride-audio/README.md).

## Dentenberg autumn textures

The local installation contains `src/MTBIndoorBern/Scene/courses/al-dente/textures/forest-floor.jpg`, a sample extracted from the owner's recording after rotating it 180°. This asset remains private and is excluded from the public repository. The renderer corrects broad lighting bands in the crop and tiles it across the ground. If the file is absent, it generates an earth-and-leaf texture in code without using video pixels. The local asset's origin and crop are recorded in `appearance.json`.

`src/MTBIndoorBern/Scene/courses/al-dente/textures/beech-branch.png` is an RGBA texture generated with the integrated ImageGen tool for the recreated trees and shrubs. It is neither a photograph nor a measurement of the route's exact vegetation. The alpha channel is preserved. The elevation profile and trail position do not depend on this texture.

Prompt used:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: transparent alpha foliage card texture for a real-time mountain biking game set in Bern woodland in late autumn. Generate ONE botanically plausible natural irregular spray of thin beech twigs with many small crisp individual ochre, muted golden yellow, tan and brown beech leaves, a few desaturated olive leaves, loosely branching in all directions from a fine central woody branch. Front orthographic view; flat soft overcast illumination, no baked directional cast shadow; realistic leaf veins, fine serrated edges and variation, photographic natural detail. An irregular airy silhouette, visible transparent gaps between groups and individual leaves; no circular blob outline. The spray occupies 85% of a square image with generous transparent margins. No trunk, no full tree, no scenery, no ground, no objects, no text or labels. Truly transparent background, preserve alpha. Avoid luminous saturation, illustration, cartoon, repeated identical leaves.

Outside the El Tony additions described above, the remaining vegetation, asphalt and gravel textures are generated in code. Estimated widths, season and sections can be adjusted in `appearance.json`. Individual obstacles seen on a section that does not match the course have not been transferred as though their positions were surveyed.
