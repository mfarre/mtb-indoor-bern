Magnetic field warning?

A detected magnet can still give an angle when its field is weak or strong. You can test and save provisional travel, while the amber warning stays visible. Check the magnet's centring and distance, then repeat both endpoints. A missing magnet, failed read or stale connection makes live travel unavailable.
